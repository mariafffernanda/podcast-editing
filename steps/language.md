# Language

- Read the session's language from the transcript and work in it: fillers, CTAs, captions and reports in that language.
- Fillers cut automatically: English um, uh, er, eh, hmm, mm; Spanish eh, em, mmm, hmm. Words that are sometimes fillers are listed for a human, never cut by themselves: English like, so, you know, I mean; Spanish este, o sea, bueno, pues.
- Talk to the user in their own language.
- In a bilingual session, each piece follows the language spoken in it.
